Pirate Map

The world is full of vandals and it has a lot of religious people. When those two inclinations meet, the result is sometimes the defacement of public property. Imagine the mental contortions one has to go through to believe that 1) there is a wise and powerful deity, and 2) it is so inept that the best idea it has to get people's attention is a cheap slogan on the side of the road, glimpsed for half a second.

We shouldn't tolerate this. Join us in keeping track of these eyesores, and help us restore our public property to being clear of this vandalism. Add pinpoints to our map and signal us when you remove rubbish.
